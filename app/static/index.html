    Zotero

    Brings in your Zotero items with their PDFs, tags, notes and highlights. Choose the folder that contains zotero.sqlite.

    Calibre

    Brings in your Calibre books with their files, covers, tags and descriptions. Choose the folder that contains metadata.db.

    Needs an internet connection. Everything else in Babel's Library works offline.

    Library folder

    Every file Babel's Library manages lives here: Articles, Books, Graphic Novels, Music (by artist and album), TV (by series and season) and Movies. You can switch to a different folder at any time.

    Automatic updates

    Book catalogue

    Two free keys add more to the catalogue's “Hot off the press” and to each book's “More info” page. Both are optional.

    The bestseller lists and NYT reviews. Get one at developer.nytimes.com: sign in, choose Apps → New App, switch on “Books API”, save, and copy the key.

    New releases with exact dates, ratings and readers' reviews. Get it at hardcover.app: sign in, open Settings → Hardcover API, and copy the token.

    News

    Your search sites

    Websites of your own choosing, shown as buttons on a catalogue entry's “More info” page next to the other links. Put {q} in the address where the title goes (and {author} for the author, if the site wants it), then tick the departments it's for. They open in their own window.

    Example: https://www.example.com/search?q={q}

    Desktop widget

    Babel's Library is either this full window or the widget. Drag the widget by its title bar, then use its pin to fix it in place. Other windows can sit in front of it. Double-click it to come back here full screen.

    Phone
